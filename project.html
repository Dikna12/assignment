MUSIC STREAMING INSIGHTS

Music streaming analytics provides a comprehensive view of your music’s performance,
allowing you to measure various aspects such as listening now, plays, average daily listeners, purchases, Shazams, milestones, and video views. This data enables you to analyze trends, track your audience’s preferences, and refine your strategies accordingly. It’s like having a powerful tool in your hands that unveils the secrets of your music’s impact and helps you navigate the ever-evolving world of music streaming.
